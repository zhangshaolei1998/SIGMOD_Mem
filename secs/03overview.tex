\section{Architecture}
\label{sec:overview}

\input{figures/tex/framework_overview}

\sys{} separates User--Agent history from Agent--Memory experience.
Query-induced first-order facts connect them: conversations supply
evidence, and execution records how that evidence is provided and used.

\subsection{From Sessions to Facts to Use Experience}
\label{subsec:new-state}

For each user, the memory state is
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:new-state}
\Mm=(\Hh,\Ff,\Uu).
\end{equation}
The progression $\Hh\rightarrow\Ff\rightarrow\Uu$ links conversations,
facts, and fact-use experience, independently of execution order.
$\Uu$ retains outcomes and costs even for empty results. All levels
are isolated by user.

\runin{Raw sessions: User--Agent history}
$\Hh=(s_1,\ldots,s_n)$ is an append-only sequence of closed,
timestamped conversations, including questions $Q$, answers $A$, and
speaker identities. Sessions remain available, including details that
no previous query needed. Agent text retains its speaker identity:
suggestions and generated claims are not independent user evidence.
They require user confirmation or lineage to already grounded assertions;
storing an answer does not create new source support.

\runin{First-order memory: what the agent knows about the user}
$\Ff$ contains source-backed assertion records $f$, normally with a
$(\mathrm{subject},\mathrm{predicate},\mathrm{object})$ payload.
Each record has a stable fact-version ID, source spans, and temporal
qualifiers; equal triples with different qualifiers remain distinct.
Event identifiers connect facts about the same activity across sessions.
Extraction is query-driven: an organizer query can acquire an event's
organizer, while a later comparison acquires its duration from the same
source. Facts accumulate as the workload reveals what is needed.

\runin{Second-order memory: Agent--Memory experience}
$\Uu$ retains memory-query context, returned evidence, support outcomes,
action costs, and validated credit for agent-reported fact use.
It distinguishes exposure from reported consumption and stores both
execution records and statistics derived from them. Repeated organizer
requests teach the system which event links to prioritize and reuse.
Observed action outcomes and costs inform subsequent execution decisions.

\begin{memoryexample}{Example 1: from a conversation to second-order experience}
\textbf{1. Raw conversations.} Alice says on March 2,
\emph{Ava arranged our 4-hour outdoor hike at Pine Trail.}
On March 9, \emph{Ben booked my 2-hour indoor pottery class.}
On March 16, \emph{Cara arranged our 3-hour outdoor ride at Lake Park.}

\smallskip
\textbf{2. The question determines extraction.} On March 17, Alice asks,
\emph{Whom should I contact to repeat my March outdoor activities?}
The agent requests March activities, settings, and organizers:

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}lll@{}}
Activity ID & Setting & Organizer\\
\texttt{hike-1} & outdoors & Ava\\
\texttt{pottery-1} & indoors & Ben\\
\texttt{ride-1} & outdoors & Cara\\
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
The six entries and verified three-activity scope support the response.
For example, \texttt{(hike-1, organizer, Ava)} is one fact;
unneeded durations remain in raw sessions.

\smallskip
\textbf{3. Retrieval differs from use.} The agent answers,
\emph{Ava for the hike; Cara for the ride.}
A complete use report credits all three settings, including pottery's
exclusion, and the Ava/Cara links. Ben's organizer was returned but unused.
Second-order memory records these IDs, context, and cost without
discarding unused facts.

\smallskip
\textbf{4. All three levels evolve.} The March 18 question about the
longest outdoor activity induces a memory query that acquires durations,
compares $4>3$, and resolves its organizer, Ava.
Durations enrich $\Ff$; their use and acquisition cost enrich $\Uu$.
The completed question/answer session is appended to $\Hh$ when it closes.
\end{memoryexample}

\subsection{How Content and Operations Evolve}
\label{subsec:new-evolution}

When $q$ arrives, its source snapshot is fixed and required evidence is
checked against $\Ff$.
Missing properties, links, versions, or population coverage identify
which source regions must be examined. Newly acquired facts enrich
$\Ff$ after verification. Acquisition reconciles identities, normalizes
dates and units, and retains temporal versions. Relative dates use the source
session's timestamp. Multiple valid objects can coexist; an explicit
update to a single-valued property creates a new version and preserves
its history.

After execution, $\Uu$ records $q$'s context, returned fact-version IDs
$E^r$, support diagnostics, and costs. $E^r$ includes the facts exposed
in $m$'s evidence bundle, not every fact inspected internally.
A complete agent use report identifies $q$ and its consumed fact or
derived-result IDs.
For a consumed result, memory expands its derivation to the supporting
facts, including negative evidence that excludes a candidate from a
count. Every such supporting fact is included in $E^r$.
Membership, lineage, and source/version checks against $q$'s snapshot validate the resulting
$E^u\subseteq E^r$;
each fact receives at most one use credit per query, with idempotent
updates by query and fact-version ID. Returning a result
establishes exposure; use requires a validated report. Credit records
reported consumption without attributing answer correctness to a fact.
Incomplete or missing reports leave
use unobserved; execution records are retained independently.

A closed User--Agent session is appended to $\Hh$. Later queries
revalidate cached scope and version checks affected by the new session;
old assertions and their provenance are not erased.

Figure~\ref{fig:framework_overview} connects the hierarchy to execution.
First-order facts supply source-backed values and identify missing evidence;
second-order experience supplies use priorities and action-cost estimates.
Both inputs support query planning and execution control. For performance, they guide
grounding and reuse within the selected pipeline
(Section~\ref{sec:framework}). For efficiency, they guide the amount
and location of additional execution
(Section~\ref{sec:executor}). The operations evolve through their
priorities, reuse decisions, and budgets; source evidence continues to
determine what a supported result can assert.
